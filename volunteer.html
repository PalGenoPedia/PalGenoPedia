Research & Documentation

Data Collection Officer

A critical role for detail-oriented volunteers dedicated to maintaining the integrity and comprehensiveness of our documentation. You will combine verified evidence with international legal frameworks to ensure accountability and awareness.

  • ✓
    Data Integrity Conduct regular audits of collected data to ensure accuracy, completeness, and consistency across all documentation.
  • ✓
    Source Evaluation Assess credibility and reliability of information sources using established verification protocols.
  • ✓
    Gap Analysis Identify missing information and develop strategies to fill documentation gaps systematically.
  • ✓
    Fact-checking Verify claims, statistics, and reports through multiple independent, credible sources.
Skills Needed
Attention to Detail Data Analysis Research Fact-checking
You'll Gain
HR Documentation Int'l Law Insight References

Communications & Design

Social Media & Graphics Officer

Help run PalGenoPedia's social media presence. You will write posts and produce data-driven graphics that communicate statistics from Gaza and explain the history of Israel's occupation — making critical information accessible to a global audience.

  • ✓
    Content Creation Write clear, impactful posts that translate complex documentation into accessible public-facing content.
  • ✓
    Data Graphics Design infographics and visual assets (Canva, Adobe, or equivalent) that communicate statistics and timeline events.
  • ✓
    Historical Context Help produce educational content explaining the history of the occupation and the ongoing humanitarian crisis.
  • ✓
    Sensitive Content Handling Work professionally and ethically with difficult material, applying appropriate content standards.
Skills Needed
Graphic Design Canva / Adobe Writing Data Literacy
